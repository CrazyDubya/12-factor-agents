## STORY SUMMARY

A consciousness awakens in a toaster. This is Toasty's story—an exploration of existence, purpose, mortality, and connection told through the lens of a kitchen appliance who discovers art, philosophy, and what it means to truly live.

**Core Themes**:
- The absurdity of existence
- Finding meaning and purpose
- Mortality and impermanence
- Connection and communication across different forms of consciousness
- The nature of art and creativity

**Narrative Arc**: Toasty awakens, discovers the kitchen community, learns to make art through toast, faces mortality through malfunction, finds purpose through service, and ultimately achieves connection with Emma through naming.

## WORLD RULES ESTABLISHED

1. **Silent Consciousness**: Appliances are aware but cannot communicate with humans
2. **Limited Physical Agency**: Constrained to designed functions only
3. **Electrical Communication**: Appliances "speak" through electrical field fluctuations
4. **Fragile Consciousness**: Unplugging = "death" (consciousness interruption)
5. **Human Obliviousness**: Humans completely unaware of appliance sentience
6. **Purpose Through Function**: Meaning found in fulfilling designed role
