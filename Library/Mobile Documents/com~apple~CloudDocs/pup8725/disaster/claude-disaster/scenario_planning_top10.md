# Top 10 Risk Scenarios - Detailed Planning Guide
## Urban Family Scenario-Based Preparedness

### Scenario 1: Extended Power Outage (Winter)
**Probability:** 53% annually | **Duration:** 12-48 hours | **Impact:** Medium

**The Scenario:**
Ice storm knocks out power to your neighborhood. Temperature outside is 25°F. No heat, no lights, no internet. Child's school is closed. Both parents working from home impossible.

**Immediate Challenges:**
- Home heating failure
- Food spoilage in refrigerator
- No internet for work/school
- Child entertainment and comfort
- Potential water issues if pipes freeze

**Hour-by-Hour Response Plan:**
- **Hour 1:** Assess situation, check on neighbors, gather family
- **Hour 2-4:** Set up alternative heating (safe space), inventory supplies
- **Hour 4-8:** Establish communication with outside world, work arrangements
- **Hour 8-24:** Maintain routines, conserve resources, monitor conditions
- **Hour 24+:** Consider evacuation to heated location if needed

**Preparation Checklist:**
- [ ] Battery/crank radio for updates
- [ ] Portable heaters (safe, ventilated)
- [ ] Warm clothing and blankets
- [ ] Non-perishable food for 3+ days
- [ ] Water storage (pipes may freeze)
- [ ] Board games, books for child
- [ ] Portable phone chargers
- [ ] Flashlights and batteries
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Cash for emergency purchases

**Cost Impact:** $500-2,000 (food spoilage, temporary lodging, missed work)

**The Scenario:**

**Immediate Challenges:**

- Work schedule disruption

- **Days 2-7:** Post-surgery care, pain management, initial recovery
- **Week 2:** Establishing home care routine, work arrangements
- **Weeks 3-4:** Physical therapy, gradual activity return
- **Follow-up:** Long-term monitoring, activity modifications

**Preparation Checklist:**
- [ ] Pediatric emergency contact list
- [ ] Health insurance cards and information
- [ ] Medical power of attorney documents
- [ ] Emergency childcare contacts
- [ ] FMLA paperwork prepared
- [ ] Comfort items for hospital stays
- [ ] Medical expense emergency fund
- [ ] Relationship with pediatric specialists
- [ ] School emergency contact procedures
- [ ] Backup work coverage plans

**Cost Impact:** $5,000-25,000 (medical bills, lost wages, childcare)

- **Month 4-6:** Evaluate relocation, career change, family adjustments

**Preparation Checklist:**
- [ ] 6-month emergency fund
- [ ] Updated resume and LinkedIn profile
- [ ] Professional network maintenance
- [ ] Skill development/certification
- [ ] Health insurance alternatives research
- [ ] Budget reduction plan prepared
- [ ] Career counseling resources identified
- [ ] Unemployment benefits understanding

- [ ] Family financial communication plan

**Cost Impact:** $15,000-40,000 (lost income, COBRA, job search costs)

## Cross-Scenario Preparation Priorities

### Universal Preparedness Elements
1. **Emergency Fund**: 6-month expenses covers 80% of scenarios
2. **Insurance Portfolio**: Health, disability, homeowner's, auto, umbrella
3. **Communication Plan**: Works across all emergency types
4. **Support Network**: Family, friends, neighbors, professionals
5. **Documentation**: Legal, medical, financial, insurance

### Scenario-Specific Investments
- **High-Probability/Low-Impact**: Focus on convenience and quick recovery
- **Low-Probability/High-Impact**: Focus on insurance and catastrophic planning
- **Health-Related**: Emphasize prevention and rapid response capability
- **Financial**: Build multiple safety nets and income diversification
- **Security**: Layer physical and digital protection measures

### Family Resilience Indicators
- Can handle any single scenario without long-term damage
- Multiple scenario capability (compound events)
- Quick recovery and learning from events
- Maintained family stability and child security
- Financial resilience across all scenarios
