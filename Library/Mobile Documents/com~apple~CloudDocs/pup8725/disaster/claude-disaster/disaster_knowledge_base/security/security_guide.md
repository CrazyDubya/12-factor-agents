# Urban Security & Safety Guide

This guide provides actionable steps to enhance your home security and personal safety before, during, and after a disaster.

## 1. Home Security: Hardening Your Castle

Your goal is to make your home a less attractive target and more resilient to damage.

### Before a Disaster:

*   **Reinforce Entry Points:**
    *   **Doors:** Install heavy-duty deadbolts. Use longer screws (3 inches) for the strike plates so they anchor into the door frame itself, not just the trim.
    *   **Windows:** Apply security film to your windows. This makes them harder to break and can prevent shattering. For ground-floor windows, consider installing locks or grilles.
    *   **Sliding Doors:** Place a strong bar (like a cut broom handle) in the track to prevent it from being forced open.
*   **Control Your Environment:**
    *   **Lighting:** Install motion-sensor lights around the exterior of your home.
    *   **Landscaping:** Trim bushes and trees near windows and doors to eliminate hiding spots.
    *   **Fencing:** A good fence is a significant deterrent.
*   **Know Your Utilities:**
    *   Know the location of your main shut-offs for gas, water, and electricity.
    *   Keep the necessary tools (like a wrench for the gas meter) permanently stored next to the shut-offs.

### During a Disaster:

*   **Shelter in Place:** If you are staying home, stay in an interior room away from windows.
*   **Low Profile:** Keep lights low and noise to a minimum, especially at night. You don't want to advertise that you have power or supplies when others don't.
*   **Evacuation:** If you must evacuate, lock your home. Turn off utilities only if you suspect damage or if instructed to by authorities.

## 2. Personal Safety & Situational Awareness

Your most important safety tool is your brain. Pay attention to your surroundings.

### Key Principles:

*   **Avoidance:** The best way to win a dangerous encounter is to not be in it in the first place. If a situation feels wrong, it probably is. Leave immediately.
*   **Gray Man Theory:** In a chaotic situation, your goal is to be uninteresting and unmemorable. Avoid wearing expensive clothes or flashy gear. Blend in.
*   **Trust Your Gut:** Intuition is your subconscious mind recognizing that something is wrong. Don't ignore that feeling.
*   **Travel in Groups:** There is safety in numbers. Avoid traveling alone, especially at night.

### Practical Steps:

*   **Plan Your Routes:** Know multiple ways to get to and from important locations. If one route is blocked, you need an alternative.
*   **Vary Your Routine:** Don't be predictable. If you follow the same route at the same time every day, you make yourself an easy target.
*   **Stay Informed:** Use a battery-powered or hand-crank radio to monitor local news and emergency broadcasts.
*   **De-escalation:** If you are confronted, try to de-escalate the situation verbally. Comply with demands if your life is at risk. Your possessions are not worth your life.

By taking these steps, you can significantly improve your security and safety in a chaotic urban environment.
