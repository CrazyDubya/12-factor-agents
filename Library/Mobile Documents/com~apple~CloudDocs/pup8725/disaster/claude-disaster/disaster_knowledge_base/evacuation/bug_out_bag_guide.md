# The Urban Bug-Out Bag Guide

This guide provides a comprehensive checklist for building a "bug-out bag" (also known as a Go Bag or 72-hour kit) tailored for an urban environment.

## Key Principles

*   **Discretion:** Your bag should not look "tactical." Use a common, nondescript backpack (like one a student or commuter would carry) to avoid drawing unwanted attention.
*   **Mobility:** You should be able to carry this bag comfortably for several miles. A general rule is that your bag should not weigh more than 25% of your body weight.
*   **Modularity:** Organize your supplies in smaller pouches or bags within your main backpack. This makes it easy to find what you need without unpacking everything.

## The Checklist

### 1. Water & Hydration

*   [ ] Water (1 liter per person, minimum)
*   [ ] Water Filter (e.g., Sawyer Mini, Lifestraw)
*   [ ] Water Purification Tablets
*   [ ] Metal Water Bottle (can be used to boil water)

### 2. Food & Nutrition

*   [ ] High-Calorie Energy Bars (3 days worth)
*   [ ] Dehydrated Meals
*   [ ] Spork or other utensil

### 3. Shelter & Warmth

*   [ ] Emergency Bivvy or Space Blanket
*   [ ] Tarp with Grommets
*   [ ] Wool Blanket
*   [ ] Change of Clothes (in a waterproof bag)
*   [ ] Rain Poncho

### 4. First Aid & Hygiene

*   [ ] Individual First Aid Kit (IFAK)
*   [ ] Prescription Medications (7-day supply)
*   [ ] Pain Relievers
*   [ ] Hand Sanitizer
*   [ ] Toilet Paper (remove the cardboard tube to save space)
*   [ ] Wet Wipes
*   [ ] Toothbrush and Toothpaste

### 5. Tools & Equipment

*   [ ] Multi-tool
*   [ ] Fixed-Blade Knife
*   [ ] Headlamp with Extra Batteries
*   [ ] Lighter and Ferro Rod
*   [ ] Duct Tape (wrap 10 feet around your water bottle)
*   [ ] Work Gloves
*   [ ] N95/P100 Dust Masks

### 6. Communication & Navigation

*   [ ] Hand-Crank Emergency Radio (NOAA weather alerts)
*   [ ] Power Bank for your phone
*   [ ] Whistle
*   [ ] Paper Map of Your City and State
*   [ ] Compass

### 7. Documents & Cash

*   [ ] Cash (small bills)
*   [ ] Copies of Important Documents (in a waterproof bag):
    *   [ ] ID / Passport
    *   [ ] Birth Certificates
    *   [ ] Social Security Cards
    *   [ ] Insurance Policies
    *   [ ] Deeds / Titles

**Action Item:** Build this bag *before* you need it. Check and rotate the supplies (especially food, water, and batteries) every six months.
