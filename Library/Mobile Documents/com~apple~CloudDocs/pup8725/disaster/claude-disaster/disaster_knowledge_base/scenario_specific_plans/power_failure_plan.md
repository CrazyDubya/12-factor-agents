# Scenario Plan: Extended Power Failure

This guide provides an actionable checklist for managing an extended power failure (more than 24 hours).

## Immediate Actions (First Hour)

*   [ ] **Assess the Situation:** Determine if the outage is localized to your home or is widespread. Check on your neighbors.
*   [ ] **Grab Your Gear:** Get out your flashlights, headlamps, and emergency radio.
*   [ ] **Check Your Supplies:** Do a quick inventory of your water, food, and batteries.
*   [ ] **Conserve Water:** Fill bathtubs and sinks with water. This water can be used for flushing toilets and for cleaning.
*   [ ] **Food Safety:** Keep your refrigerator and freezer doors closed as much as possible. A full freezer will keep food frozen for about 48 hours. A refrigerator will keep food cool for about 4 hours.

## First 24 Hours

*   [ ] **Power Down:** Unplug all sensitive electronics to protect them from power surges when the electricity is restored.
*   [ ] **Set Up Your Off-Grid Kitchen:** Get out your camp stove or other off-grid cooking method. Plan your meals.
*   [ ] **Manage Your Water:** Start using your stored water. If you have a water filter, identify a source of water you can use if the outage continues.
*   [ ] **Stay Informed:** Listen to your emergency radio for updates from local authorities.
*   [ ] **Check on Your Community:** Check on elderly or vulnerable neighbors. This is a good time to start building community resilience.

## Day 2 and Beyond

*   [ ] **Food Management:** If the outage continues beyond 48 hours, you will need to cook or otherwise preserve the food in your freezer. A mass cook-off with your neighbors is a great way to build morale and share resources.
*   [ ] **Sanitation:** If the water supply is affected, switch to your emergency sanitation plan (e.g., the twin-bucket toilet system).
*   [ ] **Security:** Maintain a low profile at night. Keep lights and noise to a minimum.
*   [ ] **Power Management:** If you have a generator or solar panels, use them judiciously. Only power essential devices.
*   [ ] **Mental Health:** An extended power outage can be stressful. Maintain a routine, stay busy, and check in with your family and neighbors.

This plan provides a basic framework. You should adapt it to your specific needs and location.
