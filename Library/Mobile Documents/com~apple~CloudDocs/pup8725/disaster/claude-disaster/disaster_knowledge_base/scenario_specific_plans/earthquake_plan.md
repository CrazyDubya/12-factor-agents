# Scenario Plan: Earthquake

This guide provides an actionable checklist for what to do before, during, and after an earthquake.

## Before the Earthquake: Mitigation

*   [ ] **Secure Your Space:**
    *   Anchor heavy furniture (bookshelves, dressers, etc.) to the walls.
    *   Secure your water heater to the wall studs.
    *   Use museum putty or earthquake straps to secure valuable objects on shelves.
    *   Install flexible gas and water lines.
*   [ ] **Create a Plan:**
    *   Identify safe spots in each room (under a sturdy table, against an interior wall).
    *   Establish a family meeting place outside your home.
    *   Know how to shut off your gas, water, and electricity.
*   [ ] **Build Your Kits:**
    *   Have a comprehensive first aid kit.
    *   Have a bug-out bag for each member of your family.
    *   Store at least one gallon of water per person, per day.

## During the Earthquake: Drop, Cover, and Hold On

*   **If you are indoors:**
    *   **Drop** to the ground.
    *   **Cover** your head and neck with your arms and get under a sturdy table or desk.
    *   **Hold On** to your shelter until the shaking stops.
    *   Stay away from windows, bookcases, and other heavy objects that could fall.
*   **If you are outdoors:**
    *   Move to an open area away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
    *   Drop to the ground and stay there until the shaking stops.
*   **If you are in a car:**
    *   Pull over to a clear location, away from bridges and overpasses.
    *   Stay in the car with your seatbelt fastened until the shaking stops.

## After the Earthquake: Assess and Act

*   [ ] **Check for Injuries:** Administer first aid as needed.
*   [ ] **Check for Hazards:**
    *   Look for fires.
    *   Smell for gas. If you smell gas, shut it off at the meter and open the windows.
    *   Look for structural damage.
*   [ ] **Communicate:**
    *   Listen to your emergency radio for instructions from local authorities.
    *   Use text messages to contact your out-of-state contact.
*   [ ] **Expect Aftershocks:** Be prepared for more shaking. Drop, cover, and hold on.
*   [ ] **Help Your Community:** Check on your neighbors, especially those who are elderly or have special needs.

This plan provides a basic framework. You should adapt it to your specific needs and location.
