Chapter 3: Convergence Point

Chronicle
The Convergence Point arrives with the alignment of Aethermoor's three moons. The ancient technology fully awakens, revealing its true purpose - not conquest or control, but evolution. The factions gather once more, this time not by choice but by necessity. The technology has created a challenge that none can face alone: a great mechanism at the heart of Aethermoor that requires magic, steam, nature, and knowledge working in perfect harmony.
