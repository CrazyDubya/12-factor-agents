# 5 Tip's for Better Productivity

Productivity is really important in todays fast paced world. Here are some tips that I found helpful.

## Tip 1: Use a To-Do List

To-do lists are great because they help you remember things. I use one everyday and it really helps me not forget stuff. You should try using one to.

## Tip 2: Take Breaks

Taking breaks is good. If you work to much you get tired. I take a break every hour or so and it helps me stay focused and not get burnt out.

## Tip 3: Eliminate Distractions

Distractions are bad for productivity. Turn off your phone and close social media. Its hard to focus when your always getting interrupted by notifications and things.

## Tip 4: Prioritize Your Tasks

Some tasks are more important then others. Do the important ones first. This way you get the most important things done even if you run out of time later.

## Tip 5: Get Enough Sleep

Sleep is super important!! If you dont get enough sleep you cant focus good and your productivity suffers. Try to get 7-8 hours every night.

These are just some ideas that work for me. Everyone is different so you might need to find what works best for you. Good luck with being more productive!
