**Advantages:** * **Optimal Performance:** Access to all device features and hardware, resulting in smooth and responsive apps. * **Best User Experience:** Native UI components feel familiar and intuitive to users on each platform. * **Access to Platform-Specific APIs:** Utilize unique functionalities offered by iOS or Android. * **Disadvantages:** * **Longest Development Time:** Requires technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), allowing for rapid development and deployment. * **Lowest Cost:** Minimal development resources needed compared to native or hybrid apps. * **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Works seamlessly across all devices with a modern browser, eliminating the need for separate app stores. * **Disadvantages:** * **Limited Offline Functionality:** While PWAs can offer resource-intensive applications. * **User Perception:** Some users may perceive PWAs as less reliable or feature-rich compared to dedicated mobile apps. Ultimately, the best choice depends on your specific project requirements, budget, and target audience. Consider the trade-offs carefully before making a decision.
